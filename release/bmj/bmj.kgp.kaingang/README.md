Kaingang lexical model
======================

Copyright © Bruno Maia de Jesus - TJ

Description
-----------
Word suggestions and corrections for Kaingang (`kgp`), for the Kaingang keyboard. The glottal stop is the
apostrophe (U+0027) and is part of the word (pã'i). Search ignores diacritics and apostrophes, so typing "ty"
suggests "tỹ" and "pai" suggests "pã'i".

Sugestões de palavras em kaingang, para o teclado Kaingang.

Sources
-------
957 words with frequencies from the official Kaingang translations of the programme "Língua Indígena Viva no
Direito" (AGU, Ministry of Indigenous Peoples, IDGlobal): the 1988 Brazilian Constitution, ILO Convention 169 and
the SDGs, plus the Portuguese–Kaingang legal glossary. Official acts are not protected by copyright in Brazil
(Law 9,610/98, art. 8). Portuguese words were filtered out by letters not used in Kaingang and by a Brazilian
Portuguese frequency list; PDF substitutes were normalized (Greek ῖ → ĩ, ᾿ and ’ → ').
