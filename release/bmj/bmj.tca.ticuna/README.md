Ticuna (Brazil) lexical model
=============================

Version 1.0

Description
-----------
Word suggestions and corrections for Ticuna (Tikuna, Magüta; `tca`) as written in Brazil, for the Ticuna keyboard.
The glottal stop is the apostrophe (U+0027) and is part of the word (nü'ü̃, naca'). Search ignores diacritics and
apostrophes, so typing "nuu" suggests "nü'ü̃".

Sugestões de palavras em ticuna na grafia brasileira, para o teclado Ticuna.

Sources
-------
2,343 words with real frequencies from official Ticuna texts translated by Ticuna translators:
- Estatuto da Criança e do Adolescente / Estatuto da Juventude in Tikuna (MDHC, OEI and UnB/LALLI, 2025).
- ILO Convention 169, SDGs and the 1988 Brazilian Constitution in Tikuna (AGU, Ministry of Indigenous Peoples,
  IDGlobal).
Official acts are not protected by copyright in Brazil (Law 9,610/98, art. 8). Portuguese words mixed in the texts
were filtered out; PDF typing substitutes were normalized (ǖ → ü̃, ’ and › → ').
